How intake works

1. The mailbox poller reads new messages from the linked IMAP folder without marking them unread.

2. Duplicates, autoresponders and bulk mail are ignored and logged.

3. Routing rules set priority, category and the technician; otherwise round robin picks the next agent.

4. The client receives the ticket reference and, when assigned, the technician's name and status link.

5. Replies thread back onto the ticket by HD-#### subject or message headers.
